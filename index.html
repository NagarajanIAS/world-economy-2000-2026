The World Economy 2000–2026

What happened to the world economy over a quarter of a century, why it happened, and what the IMF and the World Bank said at the time, including where they were wrong.

The book is built from the IMF's World Economic Outlook, Global Financial Stability Report and Fiscal Monitor, from the World Bank's World Development Reports, and from the official data behind them. Every chart names its source and series; every claim from a report is footnoted to its edition and chapter. Figures for 2026, and for some countries 2025, are estimates or projections and are marked as such. Every chapter has a section on India.

An independent study, made with Claude (Anthropic) at the author's direction. Text and charts under CC BY 4.0; see About.

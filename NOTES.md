## Theme 1, Growth and convergence (22 Nov 2026)
- Method. Shares of world output (PPPSH), income per person relative to the US (PPPPC ratio) and growth (NGDP_RPCH) come from the WEO October 2026 database; values after 2025 are IMF projections and are shaded. Period growth rates are simple averages of annual rates (2000–07, 2010–19, 2020–25, 2026–31). Figures: `scripts/derive_theme_growth.py` → `theme_growth.json` and `tg_fcst5.js`.
- Convergence count (chart T1.3). The base is the 145 economies in both the WEO database and WDI NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD whose income per person was below half the US level in 2000. The share is those whose compound annual growth of income per person beat the US's over each period. A different cut-off or data set would give different shares; the falling pattern is the point.
- Five-year-ahead forecasts (chart T1.5) are the growth forecast for year t+5 in each spring and autumn WEO, from weohistorical.xlsx (spring 2000 to autumn 2022) plus the October 2025 and April 2026 vintages and the October 2026 database. The spring 2023 to spring 2025 editions are not in either source, so the line has a gap there.
- India's figures are fiscal years in the WEO; chart T1.6 uses WDI calendar-year data. The Economic Survey's trend-growth estimates are quoted as each edition states them.
- Not obtained: WEO April 2015 Ch 3 "Where Are We Headed? Perspectives on Potential Output" and WEO September 2006 Ch 3 "Asia Rising" (all PDF links 404). Their findings are not used. Source notes: `sources/reading-notes/Theme_growth.md` (agent).
